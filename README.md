# Mes Factures - Driver Invoice & Agenda Manager

A professional, Material 3-powered Android application tailored for private drivers and small businesses. Manage your clients, track your income/expenses, schedule trips, and generate high-quality PDF invoices or order forms seamlessly.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📄 Professional Document Generation
- **PDF Invoices & Order Forms**: Generate high-quality documents based on clean, professional templates.
- **Instant Preview & Share**: View your PDFs directly in-app and share via WhatsApp, Email, or Print.
- **Custom Templates**: Choose between multiple visual styles for your documents.

### 📊 Business Intelligence & Analytics
- **Unified Dashboard**: A high-level overview of your monthly profit, invoices count, and upcoming trips.
- **Interactive Charts**: Visualize your revenue trends with dynamic bar charts (Day/Month/Year).
- **Expense Tracking**: Manage your business costs to calculate real net profit.
- **Global Activity Log**: A single timeline merging all your documents and courses for total traceability.

### 📅 Agenda & Client Management
- **Trip Scheduler**: A built-in calendar to plan your future courses and keep track of your schedule.
- **Client Directory**: Save regular customers with their legal details (SIREN, TVA) for lightning-fast billing.
- **Contact Import**: Import clients directly from your phone's contact list with smart auto-fill.
- **Revenue per Client**: View the total value generated by each specific customer at a glance.

### 🎨 Modern UI & Customization
- **Material 3 Design**: Fully compliant with modern Android design standards.
- **Dynamic Colors (Material You)**: App theme adapts to your phone's wallpaper (Android 12+).
- **Tabler Icons**: Uses the premium [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons) set for a unified, clean aesthetic.
- **Dark Mode**: A professional OLED-optimized dark theme.

### 🛡️ Security & Reliability
- **Biometric Lock**: Secure your financial data with Fingerprint or Face ID.
- **Backup & Restore**: Export all your data (database + PDFs) into a single ZIP file for easy migration or safety.
- **Offline First**: Works perfectly without an internet connection.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Language**: Java / Kotlin (Gradle DSL)
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Jetpack Components (ViewModel, LiveData, Navigation).
- **Database**: [Room Persistence Library](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room) for local storage.
- **UI/UX**: Material 3, [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons), [Lottie](https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-android).
- **Analytics**: [MPAndroidChart](https://github.com/PhilJay/MPAndroidChart) for data visualization.
- **PDF Engine**: [PDFBox-Android](https://github.com/TomRoush/PdfBox-Android) for document generation.
- **Celebrations**: [Konfetti](https://github.com/DanielMartinus/Konfetti) for rewarding user actions.

## 📥 Installation

1. **Prerequisites**:
    - Android Studio Ladybug (or newer).
    - Android SDK 36 (Android 16 preview) for compilation.
    - A device or emulator running Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher.

2. **Setup**:
    - Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/yourusername/androidInvoicePdf.git`
    - Open the project in Android Studio.
    - Sync Gradle and run the `:app` module.

## 📖 Usage

1. **Profile Setup**: Go to **Entreprise** in shortcuts to set your SIREN, TVA, and legal info.
2. **Import Clients**: Open the **Clients** tab and use the Speed Dial FAB to import from your contacts.
3. **Daily Routine**:
    - Schedule trips in the **Agenda**.
    - Generate an **Invoice** or **Bon** after a trip.
    - Check your **Dashboard** to see your profit growth.
4. **Safety**: Regularly use the **Export** feature in Settings to keep a ZIP backup of your business data.
